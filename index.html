18 Years Inside the System

Levan began his career as an intern and prosecutor at the Office of the Prosecutor General of Georgia (2006–2012), rising to Head of the Legal Department and then Head of the Department of Procedural Supervision over Investigation — overseeing procedural supervision across the Ministry of Internal Affairs, Special Foreign Intelligence Service, Ministry of Defense, and Ministry of Corrections. That vantage point now informs every defense he mounts.

Giorgi Bachiashvili — Financial Crime Defense

Serving as Georgian defense counsel to Giorgi Bachiashvili, founder of the investment firm Mission Gate, in a high-profile cryptocurrency financial-crime prosecution that has drawn sustained domestic and international media coverage.

Training the Next Generation

International Standards
