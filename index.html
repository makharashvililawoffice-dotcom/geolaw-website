Practice Areas

Makharashvili Law Office handles all types of cases — criminal law, civil law, administrative law, constitutional law, and beyond — bringing 20+ years of courtroom experience to every matter, with particular depth in criminal defense and procedure.

Criminal Defense

Representation at every stage of criminal proceedings — investigation, pre-trial motions, trial, and appeal — drawing on direct experience inside the Office of the Prosecutor General.

Jury Trial Advocacy

Certified trainer in jury trial skills; applies advanced case-preparation, witness examination, and closing-argument technique developed alongside U.S. judges and trial lawyers.

Juvenile Justice

Defense and procedural guidance in cases involving minors, informed by ongoing training delivered to fellow members of the Georgian Bar Association.

White-Collar & Financial Crime

Defense in complex financial, asset, and money-laundering allegations, including cross-border evidentiary disputes.

Civil Litigation

Civil and administrative disputes, contract matters, and general litigation support for individuals and businesses.

International Legal Cooperation

Mutual legal assistance, extradition, and transfer-of-proceedings matters, drawing on former leadership of international cooperation at the Prosecutor General’s Office.

Administrative Law

Disputes with state bodies, licensing and permitting matters, and administrative-court litigation against public agencies.

Constitutional Law

Litigation involving constitutional rights, procedural safeguards, and fundamental freedoms before the Georgian courts.

20 Years Inside the System

Levan began his career as an intern and prosecutor at the Office of the Prosecutor General of Georgia (2006–2012), rising to Head of the Legal Department and then Head of the Department of Procedural Supervision over Investigation — overseeing procedural supervision across the Ministry of Internal Affairs, Special Foreign Intelligence Service, Ministry of Defense, and Ministry of Corrections. That vantage point now informs every defense he mounts.

Giorgi Bachiashvili — Financial Crime Defense

Serving as Georgian defense counsel to Giorgi Bachiashvili, founder of the investment firm Mission Gate, in a high-profile cryptocurrency financial-crime prosecution that has drawn sustained domestic and international media coverage.

Training the Next Generation

As a Member of the Executive Board of the Georgian Bar Association, former Chairperson of its Jury Trial Working Group (2022–2025), and a certified Bar Association trainer, Levan shapes how Georgian lawyers approach criminal procedure, juvenile justice, and jury trial advocacy nationwide.

International Standards

Study visits and trial-advocacy training in the United States (Florida, 2022 & 2023) alongside American judges and public defenders, applied directly to case strategy and courtroom technique in Georgia.
